Technique & Genre Breakdown

Metal

Metal & Heavy Riffing

Learn down-picking precision, aggressive fingerstyle attack, low-end tone sculpting, and handling drop tunings (Drop D, Drop C, Low B).

  • Tuning: Drop D, Drop C, Low B
  • Tone: High mids, distortion/drive blend
  • Focus: Lock with double-bass kick drums
Rock

Rock & Hard Rock Lines

Master solid root-five groove driving, steady Eighth-note pulse, pick technique dynamics, and bridging rhythm guitar with drums.

  • Tuning: Standard E, Drop D
  • Tone: Warm punch, classic P-bass tone
  • Focus: Consistent dynamic level and groove
Jazz

Jazz & Walking Bass

Construct walking basslines using voice leading, arpeggios, chromatic passing tones, and fluid finger technique with smooth tone.

  • Tuning: Standard E (4-String / 5-String)
  • Tone: Warm neck pickup, soft finger touch
  • Focus: Harmonic progression & swing feel
